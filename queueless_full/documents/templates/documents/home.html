Is it free to use?

Making an account and sending a request is free. Some documents may have a fee at the barangay hall.

What do I bring when I pick up?

Bring a valid ID and your reference code. You can find the code on your My Requests page.

How long does it take?

Staff will check your request soon. You can watch the progress bar on your request.
